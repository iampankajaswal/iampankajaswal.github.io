What this site collects

Nothing. This site is composed entirely of static files. It sets no cookies, contains no analytics, no tag managers, no advertising or tracking pixels, no embedded social widgets, no contact form and no externally hosted fonts or scripts. GitHub's own privacy statement governs this processing and is available at docs.github.com.
