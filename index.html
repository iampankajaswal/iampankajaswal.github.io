Profile

Ten years in, my work has settled into a consistent shape: I am the person who owns the platform. That has meant standing up multi-cloud Terraform estates from nothing, running Kubernetes where downtime is a regulatory event, and building the observability that turns an outage from a mystery into a timeline.

Most of that time has been inside constraints — RBI banking compliance at Paytm Payments Bank, audit-ready infrastructure state, least-privilege access as a default rather than a retrofit. I have found those constraints make for better engineering. Policy-as-code in every pipeline is not overhead once it is the only path to production.

I am now looking for a senior platform, SRE or DevOps role in Europe. What I want is ownership of a real platform problem, a team that treats reliability as a product concern, and the room to do the unglamorous work properly.
